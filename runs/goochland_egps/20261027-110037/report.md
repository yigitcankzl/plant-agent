# Technical Memorandum — Eastern Goochland Pump Station and Force Main (48-inch FRP replacement, Section B), Goochland County, Virginia

## Purpose
Evaluate the post-rupture Section B replacement — first ~2.5 miles (about 10,755 ft) from Eastern Goochland Pump Station (EGPS) with 42-in IPS DR-21 HDPE (37.76-in ID) (p.29) — with remaining FRP and ductile iron (DI) in place. Determine for the smaller pipe whether EGPS can meet current required capacity 14.06 MGD (~14.1 MGD) with 2 duty pumps and 2045 requirement 27.09 MGD (~27.1 MGD) with 5 duty pumps (p.31), for every operating mode: to Richmond outfall, to Henrico outfall, and to Henrico while Gambles Mill Pump Station also pumps. Report flows, heads, steady pressures and velocities. Check pump-station power-failure trip with all duty pumps running, now and as station grows to 3, 4 and 5 duty pumps, against allowable surge pressures of HDPE and remaining FRP and DI. Recommend County action.

Source: Dewberry for Goochland County Department of Public Utilities, Eastern Goochland Force Main Replacement - Section B, Preliminary Engineering Report, December 29, 2021 (public record) (p.9, p.28, p.29, p.31, p.38, p.39, p.40, p.41).

# Step 1 - Scope and design criteria

**Question:** The City will divert Albert Whitted's flow to the Southwest WRF, and the projected peak wet weather flow is 69 MGD. Using the hydraulic profile (Figure 5-1, water surfaces modelled at 20 and 40 MGD) and the facility data, what is the plant's hydraulic capacity today (the flow at which a tank first overtops its wall), which structure limits it, which hydraulic elements fail at 69 MGD, and what modifications or flow management would let the plant handle the 69 MGD peak?

## Criteria
| name | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| Permitted treatment capacity (AADF) | 20 MGD annual average daily flow | p.8 Sec 1; p.10 Sec 2.1 |
| Study target - future peak wet weather (peak hour) influent to SWWRF after AWWRF diversion | 69.0 MGD Peak Hour influent (52.6 MGD Max Day, 31.4 MGD Max Month, 20.0 MGD Annual Average) | p.22 Table 3-3; p.29 Sec 5.2; question statement 69 MGD |
| Hydraulic capacity definition | flow at which wastewater would overtopple the top of wall for that tank | p.29 Sec 5.2 |
| Hydraulic profile basis - modelled flows shown on Figure 5-1 | water surfaces modelled at 20 and 40 MGD after Biosolids to Energy improvements; 40 MGD not hydraulically challenged | p.29 Sec 5.2; question statement; dwg p.30 title |
| Reported hydraulic capacity and limiting unit (to be verified by re-model in later steps) | 40.5 MGD limited by the filters | p.29 Sec 5.2 |
| Profile conservatism - flow path | most conservative path excluding old plant: through secondary clarifier #3 (higher V-notch weir than #1 and #2) and northern-most filter (longest path) | p.28 Sec 5 |
| Profile conservatism - filter level | hydraulic elevation in filters maintained at filter overflow elevation 115.3 feet to represent blinded filters | p.28 Sec 5 |
| Profile RAS assumption | RAS flowrate 20 MGD used in profile model | dwg p.30 notes (read_drawing) |
| Wet-weather loading assumption for process capacity | pollutant loadings during wet weather similar to dry weather loadings (dilution, concentrations drop below 80 mg/L CBOD5/TSS at peak) | p.21 Sec 3.3 |
| Basis of 69 MGD projection | 25.5 MGD max dry weather flow + 43.2 MGD max storm contribution = 68.7 MGD rounded to 69.0 MGD peak hour | p.20 Sec 3.1 |

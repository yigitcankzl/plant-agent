# Step 1 - Scope and design criteria

**Question:** Township operating record (from the client, not in the visible pages): the existing 18-inch cast iron force main broke in the past at an operating pressure of about 90 psi. The Rittenhouse Boulevard sewage pump station must convey the projected 20-year peak flow of 4,960 gpm (7.14 MGD) with one pump out of service. Can the existing station and force main do it? If not, what limits it, and what are the least-cost alternatives (pumps, impellers, force main) that achieve at least 4,960 gpm with two pumps operating? Verify each alternative's operating point.

# Step 1 - Scope and design criteria

**Question:** The Rittenhouse Boulevard sewage pump station must convey the projected 20-year peak flow of 4,960 gpm (7.14 MGD) with one pump out of service. Can the existing station and force main do it? If not, what limits it, and what are the least-cost alternatives (pumps, impellers, force main) that achieve at least 4,960 gpm with two pumps operating? Verify each alternative's operating point.

| name | value | source |
|---|---|---|

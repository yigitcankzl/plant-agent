# Step 1 - Scope and design criteria

**Question:** A new finished water pump station at the regional WTP will feed two long steel transmission mains: about 41,000 ft of 42-inch to the Turlock terminal tank and about 29,000 ft of 30-inch to the Ceres terminal tank. If the pump station loses power while running at design flow (Phase 1: 10 mgd Turlock, 5 mgd Ceres; buildout: 30 mgd and 15 mgd), what happens to pressure along the pipelines before the standby generator restarts the pumps? Is surge protection needed to keep the regulatory pressure margin, and if so, what type and size (volume and air ratio) for each pipeline in Phase 1 and at buildout? Note anything the design-build team could change to reduce it.

## Criteria
| name | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum transient pressure - finished water system | 10 psi minimum | p.3 - State Water Resources Control Board, DDW, 2018 |
| High-pressure surge risk criterion - steel pipe | steel pipeline has surge allowance of 150 percent of design pressure; mitigation for low pressures will reduce highest pressures to close to normal operating conditions | p.3 |
| Transient scenarios to evaluate - power failure | evaluate low-pressure event and water column separation following abrupt power failure and subsequent high-pressure surge on rejoin, and maintaining pressurized flow to meet regulatory requirements and avoid pathogen intrusion | p.2 |
| Phase 1 design flow condition | 15 mgd total: 10 mgd to Turlock terminal tank, 5 mgd to Ceres terminal tank | p.3 |
| Buildout design flow condition | 45 mgd total: 30 mgd to Turlock terminal tank, 15 mgd to Ceres terminal tank | p.3 |
| Pump restart sequence after power failure | Generator start 10 seconds after power failure; first pump starts with generator and ramps to full speed in 30 seconds; each subsequent pump ramps in 30 seconds with 30 second lag before next pump starts | p.2-p.3, p.7 Figure 2 |

## Units on the flow path
- Regional WTP clearwell (WS El. 114 ft)
- Finished Water Pump Station (El. 107 ft) - Turlock pumps + Ceres pumps with VFDs
- Turlock Finished Water Pump Station - Phase 1: 3 pumps (2 duty, 1 standby); Buildout: 7 pumps (6 duty, 1 standby); 3,500 gpm per pump
- Ceres Finished Water Pump Station - Phase 1: 2 pumps (1 duty, 1 standby); Buildout: 4 pumps (3 duty, 1 standby); 3,500 gpm per pump
- Turlock Finished Water Transmission Main - ~41,000 LF 42-inch cement mortar lined and coated steel pipe WTP to Turlock terminal tank
- Ceres Finished Water Transmission Main - ~29,000 LF 30-inch cement mortar lined and coated steel pipe WTP to Ceres terminal tank
- Altitude valves with sustaining feature at terminal tanks (35 psi / 80.9 ft above tank bottom; HGL 198 ft Turlock, 190 ft Ceres)
- Turlock terminal tank (bottom El. 117.1 ft, WS 10 ft above bottom)
- Ceres terminal tank (bottom El. 108.7 ft, WS 10 ft above bottom)
- Finished Water Pump Station Generator(s) - 10-sec start, sequential 30-sec pump ramps with 30-sec lags

# Step 1 - Scope and design criteria

**Question:** Was operating pressure or surge involved in Aug 13 2022 120-inch PCCP failure ~0.5-1 mi west of LHWTP, does rest of inspected main need immediate replacement, and what design/operation/emergency-response changes should prevent/limit repeat?

## Definitions
| term | definition | source |
|---|---|---|
| PCCP | Prestressed concrete cylinder pipe with steel cylinder, concrete and prestress wire wrapped outside to withstand design pressure; embedded type in this main | p.7, p.126 |
| Wire-break (WB) region | Electromagnetic wire-break detection: PICA Near-Field Testing (NFT) and Pure PipeWalker; regions reported as estimated number of broken prestress wire wraps; uncalibrated estimates where no pipe-specific calibration performed | p.15, p.146, p.174 |
| Anomaly - manufacturing vs anomalous joint | Small-magnitude EM signal with same phase/amplitude traits suspected related to manufacturing vs joint geometry responses (anomalous joints) | p.135 (p.126 summary) |
| Firm capacity | Capacity with largest unit of a station out of service - applied to LHWTP / IMC pumping and to transmission redundancy (sole conduit vs looped/emergency-feed capability) | document units rule; p.9 sole conduit statement |

## Criteria
| name | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| Normal operating / design pressure limit for 120-inch main | up to 200 psi normal operating; 200 psi design - operating pressure did not exceed design immediately leading up to event | p.7 and p.17 |
| Water emergency and EGLE reporting basis | loss of pressure / lack of adequate supply = water emergency requiring discovery, cause, corrective actions, public notice, affected area, duration and coping ability report | p.16 (Rule R325.10104(m), Rule R325.12304) |
| Repair disinfection and return-to-service test | fill/chlorinate/flush, chlorine residuals to normal, pressure testing, 3 consecutive days bacteriological samples passing before BWA lift and restoration to service | p.18-p.19 (R325.11109-10; AWWA C651-14 slug method; PA 399) |
| Post-break electromagnetic condition assessment extent | dewatered main inspected: PICA NFT ~1.58 miles at break (0.5 mi east + 1.0 mi west); Pure PipeWalker 0.62 mi east + 3.77 mi west; visual/sounding for imminent failure; wire-break regions quantified | p.14-p.15, p.126, p.144, p.172 |
| PCCP distress screening for this study | inventory distress: PICA 7 WB regions max 15 WBs, 0 regions >15 WBs; Pure West 27/1248 pipes with broken wraps; use max count, spatial clustering and visual distress to judge immediate replacement need - no >15-WB region found in inspected length | p.126, p.134, p.169 (PICA + Pure results); no agency threshold stated in available pages |

# Step 1 - Scope and design criteria

**Question:** Can the Winchester WTP be expanded from its current 12 mgd to 18 mgd? For each unit along the flow path, determine whether it limits 18 mgd, the flow at which it becomes limiting, and the least-cost modification that makes 18 mgd work.

## Definitions
| term | definition | source |
|---|---|---|
| Hydraulic capacity | Related to piping, pumping, volume and flow control systems which limit ability of water to flow through individual units and interconnected system | p.107 |
| Treatment capacity | Ability of each treatment unit process to meet regulatory requirements | p.107 |
| Firm capacity | Capacity with largest unit out of service; HSPS nominally 10 mgd; WWPS ~3000 gpm with one of three pumps out | p.115 + p.119 |
| Clearwell storage volume | Total maximum 627,000 gal at 10.5 ft depth; 75 min (1.26 hr) at 12 mgd; plus 85,000 gal under Filters 5-6 for 57 min at 18 mgd | p.114-p.115 |

## Criteria
| name | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| Current plant nominal treatment rating | 12 mgd single flocculation/sedimentation basin rated at 12 mgd | p.111 |
| Expansion target flow for this study | 18 mgd expanded plant capacity with two additional filters (Filters 5 and 6) | p.115 |
| Drawing ultimate design flow | 22 MGD ultimate plant flow note on WINCHESTER WTP HYDRAULIC PROFILE | dwg p.136 |
| Ultimate overflow / planning flow | 24 mgd combined clearwell overflows can accommodate ultimate plant flow | p.114 |
| Firm capacity definition | Firm capacity = capacity with largest unit out of service; e.g. high service firm nominally 10 mgd, wastewater firm 3000 gpm with one pump out | Engineering rules + p.115, p.119 |
| Desirable clearwell detention | ~60 minutes detention at peak flow; currently met at 12 mgd only if entire clearwell volume available; 57 min at 18 mgd with Filters 5-6 | p.115 |
| Desirable transmission main velocity | ~6.0 fps maximum desirable velocity in finished water main; short on-site segment allows higher as acceptable; 6.0 fps exceeded at ~18 mgd | p.116 |
| Surge protection rating | Surge protection provided for pumping rates up to 9700 gpm (14.0 mgd); upsurge to 150 psi, downsurge to HGL 630 ft | p.117 |
| Clearwell overflow rating | HSPS weir El.449.5 ft x 9 ft ~19 mgd at 1 ft depth; Filter No.3 weir El.450.00 ft x 15 ft; combined 24 mgd; normal operation 8-10 ft depth, 3 to 1 ft below overflow | p.114 |
| Filter normal operating level | 463 to 464 ft controlled by effluent flow control; 5 to 6 ft submergence over media | p.112 |
| Filter-to-waste demonstrated capacity | ~2 mgd does not appear to convey more than 2 mgd; difference vs filter design flow causes flow surge on other filters | p.113 |
| Demonstrated pretreatment performance | Excellent pretreatment at sustained flows up to 11.5 mgd warm low-turbidity; normal cold high-turbidity 3.5-5.0 mgd | p.111 |
| Plant operating range observed | 3.5 mgd low demand to 11.6 mgd recent peak summer day, operated 24/7 to keep Reservoir Hill terminal reservoirs full | p.108 |

# Step 1 - Scope and design criteria

**Question:** Can the Winchester Water Treatment Plant be expanded from its current 12 mgd to 18 mgd? For each unit along the flow path, determine whether it limits 18 mgd, the flow at which it becomes limiting, and the least-cost modification that makes 18 mgd work.

## Definitions
_none_

## Criteria
| name | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| Current rated plant capacity | 12 mgd - treatment capacity limitation determined by the four existing filters | p.107 |
| Planned expansion nominal capacity | 18 mgd - treatment capacity limitation based on ultimate buildout of six filters using same media as existing four | p.107 |
| Hydraulic vs treatment capacity distinction | Hydraulic capacity is piping, pumping, volume and flow control limits; treatment capacity is ability to meet regulatory or industry standards | p.107 |
| Hydraulic profile design basis | Developed during original design for maximum instantaneous flow of 18 mgd; updated based on field tests for maximum flow of 22 mgd | p.108 |
| Firm pumping capacity definition | Maximum flow achievable with one of the largest installed pumps out of service | p.108 |
| Maximum desirable pipeline velocity | Typically 6.0 fps in transmission main of this type; short segment of finished main allows higher as acceptable | p.110, p.116 |
| Fish protection approach velocity | Maximum approach velocity 0.4 fps applies at 4-ft square gated openings outside fish screens, gates fully submerged | p.109 |
| Clearwell storage guideline | Generally desirable minimum ~60 minutes detention at peak flow rate | p.115 |
| Surge protection performance criteria | Protection to 9700 gpm (14.0 mgd); max upsurge 150 psi; min downsurge at HS station HGL 630 ft (~175 ft head or 75 psi assuming ground 455 ft) | p.117 |
| Filter treatment rating basis | 3 mgd per filter treatment (12 mgd /4 filters; 18 mgd /6 filters same media); hydraulically 4 mgd per filter filtering to clearwell | p.107, p.112 |
| Water rights availability | City has 20 mgd on North Umpqua River, exceeds 18 mgd; additional rights investigated | p.107 |

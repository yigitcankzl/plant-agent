# Step 1 - Scope and design criteria

**Question:** Can the Winchester Water Treatment Plant be expanded from its current 12 mgd to 18 mgd? For each unit along the flow path, determine whether it limits 18 mgd, the flow at which it becomes limiting, and the least-cost modification that makes 18 mgd work.

## Criteria
| name | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| Current rated plant capacity | 12 mgd - treatment limitation determined by four existing filters | p.107 |
| Expansion nominal capacity target | 18 mgd - treatment limitation with ultimate buildout of six filters using same media as existing | p.107 |
| Firm pumping capacity definition | maximum flow with one of the largest installed pumps out of service | p.108 |
| Fish protection approach velocity | 0.4 fps maximum at gates outside fish screens, fully submerged | p.109 |
| Maximum desirable transmission main velocity | 6.0 fps in steel transmission main; short WTP-site segment may allow higher | p.110 p.116 |
| Floc/sed basin freeboard adequacy | approx 1.5 ft from max water surface to top of walls is adequate | p.111 |
| Filter normal operating level | 463 to 464 ft, controlled by effluent flow control; 5 to 6 ft submergence over media | p.112 |
| Filter driving head available | approximately 15 to 17.5 ft from filter water level to treated water storage level (446.5-448 ft); alarm at 11.0 ft terminal headloss | p.112 |
| Per-filter treatment rating (existing media) | 3 mgd per filter treatment limitation; hydraulically 4 mgd per filter to clearwell; 6 filters = 24 mgd hydraulic | p.112 |
| Clearwell normal limits | normal max 449.0 ft, 6-in below overflow; normally operated 446.5 to 448 ft | p.112 p.114 |
| Clearwell detention desirability criterion | minimum approx 60 minutes detention at peak flow; 1-hour criterion = 1,000,000 gal at 24 mgd | p.115 |
| Surge protection performance criteria | protection to 9,700 gpm (14.0 mgd); max upsurge 150 psi; min downsurge HGL 630 ft (approx 175 ft head or 75 psi at grade 455 ft) | p.117 |
| Water rights available | 20 mgd on North Umpqua River; additional rights investigated | p.107 |
| Hydraulic profile design flows | original design 18 mgd max instantaneous; updated Figure 4-2 for 22 mgd max; drawing note ultimate 22 mgd | p.108 (dwg p.136) |

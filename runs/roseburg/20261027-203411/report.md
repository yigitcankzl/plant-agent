# Technical Memorandum – Winchester Water Treatment Plant Expansion 12 mgd to 18 mgd

## Purpose

Determine whether the Winchester Water Treatment Plant, Roseburg, Oregon can be expanded from current nominal 12 mgd to 18 mgd (p.111), and for each unit along the flow path determine whether it limits 18 mgd, the flow at which it becomes limiting, and the least-cost modification that makes 18 mgd work.

Source: City of Roseburg, Water Treatment Facilities Preliminary Design Report, July 2009 (public record).

## Background

- Plant currently operates 24/7 to keep Reservoir Hill reservoirs full; flow ranged approximately 3.5 mgd to 11.6 mgd (p.108).
- Single existing flocculation/sedimentation basin rated at 12 mgd and designed to be expanded with parallel basin for ultimate nominal 18 mgd (p.111).
- Flow path per drawing (dwg p.136): RIVER INTAKE - RAPID MIX - FLOCCULATION BASIN - SEDIMENTATION BASIN - FILTERS - CLEARWELL - HIGH SERVICE & BACKWASH PUMPS - RESERVOIR.
- Ultimate hydraulic basis for profile drawing: BASED ON AN ULTIMATE PLANT FLOW OF 22 MGD (dwg p.136 Note 2).
- Ultimate hydraulic capacity two basins in parallel limited primarily by settled water pipeline, 22 to 24 mgd (p.112); with approximately 24 mgd hydraulic capacity (p.111).
- Report distinguishes hydraulic capacity vs treatment capacity (p.107):
  - Hydraulic = piping, pumping, volume and flow control.
  - Treatment = ability to meet regulatory requirements.

## Design Criteria

- Study target: 12 mgd existing nominal, 18 mgd future nominal (p.111).
- Ultimate hydraulic profile basis: 22 MGD (dwg p.136).
- Two-basin ultimate: 22-24 mgd hydraulic, 18 mgd nominal treatment (p.111; p.112).
- Firm capacity definition: firm = N-1 largest unit out.
- Raw-water pipeline max desirable velocity: 6.0 fps (p.110).
- Finished-water transmission velocity screen: **ASSUMPTION** – apply same 6.0 fps screen; Table 4-3 (p.117) shows 5.97 fps at 18 mgd with no exceedance stated; to be confirmed by headloss/TDH analysis.
- Intake fish-protection approach velocity: 0.4 fps at 4-ft square gated openings outside fish screens, gates fully submerged (p.109).
- Pretreatment freeboard: approximately 1.5 ft adequate (p.111).
- Basin overflow weir datum: 465.0 ft, approximately 1.0 ft higher than normal filter operating level (p.111).
- Normal filter operating level: 463 to 464 ft (p.112).
- Filter-to-waste objective: maintain relatively constant flow through all filters at all times (p.113).
- Clearwell operating levels: high 449.0 ft, normal 446.5-448.0 ft depending on hydraulic efficiency and chlorine residual (dwg p.136).
- Clearwell storage datum: 627,000 gal at 10.5 ft water depth = approximately 75 minutes (1.26 hours) at current maximum (p.114).
- Backwash redundancy: only one pump required; second serves as spare/backup – N+1 (p.118-p.119).
- Wastewater pump station firm basis: approximate 3,000 gpm with one pump out of service (p.119).
- Surge protection criteria: up to 9,700 gpm (14.0 mgd); max upsurge 150 psi; min downsurge HGL 630 ft at HSPS, approximately 175 ft head or 75 psi assuming ground 455 ft (p.117).

## Recommendations

To make 18 mgd work (least-cost, text-governed):

1. Intake: Add 5th 4x4-ft opening or equivalent + T-INT dive survey that gates fully submerged; screens marginal at up to 18 mgd or more (p.109).
2. Raw pumps: Rehab Pump 3 + VFDs/controls; prove 18 mgd firm by T-RWPS TDH test against Table 4-1 HL (p.110), else add capacity. Demonstrated 14-15 mgd (p.109) currently limits.
3. Pretreatment: Build 2nd floc/sed basin parallel + 2nd 9-ft weir (~12 mgd each) + 2nd 30-in drain (~10 mgd each) as designed (p.111) for 18 mgd nominal / 22-24 mgd hydraulic (p.111-p.112). Note two-basin drain limit 20 mgd vs weir 24 mgd.
4. Filters: Add Filters 5-6 same media for 18 mgd nominal (p.107); hold 463-464 ft (p.112); require T-FLT loading/FTW modulation test (~2 mgd waste at 9.1 mgd) (p.113) + State approval if high-rate claimed.
5. Clearwell: No volume expansion for 18 mgd (~57 min screening); must prove CT by baffling/chlorine (two zones unbaffled) (p.114) via T-CT at 446.5-448.0 ft operating, 449.0 ft high (dwg p.136).
6. HSPS/surge/transmission: Replace existing tank with larger tank (p.117) (second tank impractical p.117) + Table 4-2 Pumps 5-6 (p.116) + transient reanalysis to 150 psi / HGL 630 ft (p.117); stage WTP-to-Hill parallels (p.118); replace low-reading propeller with mag meter (p.116). Rated surge 14.0 mgd (p.117) limits.
7. Pipelines: No replacement of 30-in raw or 30-in finished site main for 18 mgd (bind just over 18 mgd by 6.0 fps screen) (p.110; p.117); confirm by T-PIPE; no modification to 42-in settled pipe (22-24 mgd) (p.112).
8. Backwash/wastewater: No backwash upgrade at 18 mgd existing media (p.119); maintain WWPS firm 3,000 gpm sequencing (p.119) and 2x300k gal basins (p.119); second overflow drain resolves N-1 drainage.

Field program before design: T-RWPS, T-INT, T-PIPE, T-SED, T-FLT, T-CT, T-HS, T-BW, T-SURV, T-CHEM.

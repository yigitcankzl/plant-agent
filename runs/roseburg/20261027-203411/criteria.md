# Step 1 - Scope and design criteria

**Question:** Can the Winchester Water Treatment Plant be expanded from its current 12 mgd to 18 mgd? For each unit along the flow path, determine whether it limits 18 mgd, the flow at which it becomes limiting, and the least-cost modification that makes 18 mgd work.

## Criteria
| name | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| Study target - expand nominal 12 mgd to 18 mgd | 12 mgd existing nominal, 18 mgd future nominal | p.111 - single existing basin is rated at 12 mgd and was designed to be expanded with a parallel basin for an ultimate nominal treatment capacity of 18 mgd |
| Ultimate hydraulic basis for profile drawing | 22 MGD hydraulic profile basis | dwg p.136 Note 2. BASED ON AN ULTIMATE PLANT FLOW OF 22 MGD. |
| Ultimate hydraulic capacity of 2-basin pretreatment | 22-24 mgd hydraulic, 18 mgd nominal treatment for 2 basins | p.111 - with approximately 24 mgd of hydraulic capacity; p.112 - ultimate hydraulic capacity of the two basins in parallel is limited primarily by the settled water pipeline and that ultimate capacity is 22 to 24 mgd |
| Firm capacity definition | firm = N-1 largest unit out | Engineering rules - Firm capacity = capacity with the largest unit of a station out of service |
| Raw-water pipeline max desirable velocity | 6.0 fps | p.110 - The maximum desirable velocity in a transmission main of this type is typically 6.0 fps. |
| Finished-water transmission velocity guide (same type main) | 6.0 fps screening | ASSUMPTION - p.110 states 6.0 fps for raw-water transmission main of this type; Table 4-3 p.117 shows 5.97 fps at 18 mgd with no exceedance stated, apply same 6.0 fps screen; to be confirmed by headloss/TDH analysis |
| Intake fish-protection approach velocity | 0.4 fps at 4-ft square gated openings outside fish screens | p.109 - maximum approach velocity for protection of fish applies at these openings. With a maximum approach velocity of 0.4 fps and with the gates fully submerged |
| Pretreatment freeboard adequacy | 1.5 ft adequate | p.111 - The freeboard from the top of maximum water surface to the top of walls is approximately 1.5 feet which is adequate. |
| Basin overflow weir datum | 465.0 ft, ~1.0 ft above normal filter level | p.111 - The overflow weir elevation is at 465.0 feet, approximately 1.0 feet higher than the normal filter operating level. |
| Normal filter operating level | 463-464 ft | p.112 - The normal filter operating level ranges from 463 to 464 feet |
| Filter-to-waste operation objective | constant flow through all filters, avoid rapid increases/shear/breakthrough | p.113 - It is desirable to modulate filter-to-waste flow to maintain relatively constant flow through all filters at all times. |
| Clearwell operating levels | high 449.0 ft, normal 446.5-448.0 ft | dwg p.136 - NORMAL HIGH WS IN CLEARWELL 449.0 NORMAL OPERATING LEVEL 446.5 TO 448.0 DEPENDING UPON HYDRAULIC EFFICIENCY AFTER EXPANSION AND UPON CHLORINE RESIDUAL MAINTAINED. |
| Clearwell storage datum at max depth | 627,000 gal at 10.5 ft = 75 min at current max (~12 mgd) | p.114 - maximum storage volume ... at 10.5 foot water depth ... total maximum ... 627,000 gallons represents approximately 75 minutes (1.26 hours) of storage at the current maximum |
| Backwash redundancy | N+1, 1 duty + 1 spare | p.118-p.119 - Only one pump is required for backwashing each of the filter bays. The second pump serves as a spare/backup |
| Wastewater pump station firm basis | 3,000 gpm firm (1 of 3 out) | p.119 - approximate firm capacity of this station is 3,000 gpm with one of the pumps out of service. |

The minimum allowable downsurge at the high service pump station is controlled to an HGL of 630 feet (approximately 175 feet of head or 75 psi assuming plant ground elevation of 455 feet.) |
| Hydraulic vs treatment capacity distinction | evaluate both hydraulics and treatment/regulatory | p.107 - The hydraulic capacity is related to the piping, pumping, volume and flow control systems ... The treatment capacity is related to the ability of each treatment unit process to meet regulatory requirements |

## Units on the flow path
- RIVER INTAKE - gated river openings, trash rack, traveling screens, pump-bay openings, raw water pumps
- RAW WATER PIPELINE - 30-in steel + 30-in mag meter to floc/sed basin NE corner
- RAPID MIX - 2 basins parallel (1 operated now, 2 at high flows)
- FLOCCULATION / SEDIMENTATION BASIN - existing 12-mgd basin + future 2nd parallel (18 mgd nominal, 24 mgd hydraulic), chain-flight, launders, settled channel, 9-ft overflow weir + 30-in drain to diversion MH
- SETTLED WATER PIPELINE - 42-in steel to filters
- FILTERS - influent channel, 20-in influent per filter, filter bays (4 implied), effluent + filter-to-waste piping
- CLEARWELL - zone 1 under filters unbaffled, zone 2 baffled under chemical/blower rooms, zone 3 unbaffled under HS room
- HIGH SERVICE PUMPS + SITE TRANSMISSION - 18- to 30-in header, 29.25-in ID buried main, hydropneumatic surge tank
- FINISHED WATER TRANSMISSION WTP TO RESERVOIR HILL - 20-in steel + 30-in DI northerly system + phased parallels to 710.0-ft reservoirs
- BACKWASH SYSTEM - air scour blower + 8-in air, 2x backwash pumps + 16-in header, 16-in drains + conveyance channel + 24-in/30-in to WWPS
- WASTEWATER PUMP STATION + DIVERSION MH (weir to river)
- SOLIDS/WASHWATER HANDLING - 2x 300,000-gal equalization/settling basins (decant) + 2 sludge drying beds + sludge transfer pumps

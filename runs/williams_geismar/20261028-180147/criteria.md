# Step 1 - Scope and design criteria

**Question:** On June 13, 2013, propylene fractionator Reboiler B at the Williams Geismar olefins plant ruptured about three minutes after an operations supervisor opened the hot quench water (about 187 F) to its tubes; it had been offline on standby for 16 months since maintenance in February 2012. Shell side process fluid: about 95% propane, balance mostly propylene and C4s. Reboiler B shell design: maximum allowable working pressure 300 psig, hydrostatic test 450 psig. Using the facility description, the schematic and the incident timeline, determine the failure mechanism and estimate with calculations the pressures that could be reached in the shell, explain why the reboiler was not protected from overpressure, and recommend the design, procedural and management changes that would prevent a recurrence.

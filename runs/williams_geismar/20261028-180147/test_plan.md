# Field test plan (closes RFI items before design)

| test | purpose | closes_rfi | measurements | procedure | acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-01 Quench water inlet temperature survey | Fix heat-source temperature for vapor-pressure and heat-input calculations | RFI-01 | Tube-side quench water inlet temperature at Reboilers A/B inlet nozzles and DCS historian 24-hr trend before incident window | Clamp-on/calibrated RTD + thermowell check at inlet; export historian Figure 5-6 data as CSV; record ambient and cooler duty | Calibrated RTD within +/-2 F of DCS; historian 1-min average stable; resolves 185 vs 187 F |
| T-02 Nameplate/datasheet/relief-record review | Anchor failure threshold (300/450 psig screening) to code record | RFI-02 | Shell/tube MAWP, design T/P, hydrotest, MDMT, relief setpoint and capacity at column-top valve and any shell reliefs | Field nameplate photos, retrieve U-1, exchanger datasheet, relief sizing calcs; bench-test or pop-record review if removed | Photographed nameplate + U-1/datasheet match; MAWP/setpoint legible; discrepancy <5 psi triggers recalc |
| T-03 Process fluid assay | Provide composition basis for vapor-pressure vs thermal-expansion estimates | RFI-03 | Shell-side liquid grab at reboiler inlet/drain + column bottoms; composition, SG, bubble point | Sample per GPA 2177/GPA 2286 GC at operating pressure; record sampling P/T; obtain EOS properties for propane-rich mix | GC repeatability within 2 mol%; bubble-point within 5 psi of calc; sample chain-of-custody complete |
| T-04 Standby lineup and initial-condition reconstruction | Confirm blocked-in liquid-full hypothesis and trapped volume | RFI-04 | Shell-side gate valve positions, tube-side valve positions, shell pressure/level/drains, nitrogen connections | Walkdown P&ID vs field lineup for standby B; retrieve DCS pressure alarms/trends; review drain/vent logs; check for level bridle/blinds | Valve positions as-found photographed; shell pressure trend recovered or bounded; fill fraction estimated +/-10% |
| T-05 Relief-path verification | Explain why standby reboiler was not protected and size corrective relief | RFI-05 | Relief valve location(s), setpoints, orifice areas, inlet/outlet piping, and 2001 gate-valve locking/car-seal state | Trace Figure 4/9/14 P&IDs in field; measure inlet line lengths; review sizing calcs per API 521 blocked-in/thermal cases; check administrative controls | P&ID redline shows continuous relief path or confirms isolation; relief capacity vs blocked-in case documented |
| T-06 Geometry and volume survey | Provide trapped-liquid volume for thermal-expansion pressure runs | RFI-06 | Shell OD/length, thickness UT grid, tube OD/count/length, nozzle/piping dead-leg volumes to isolation valves | Field tape/UT, review fab drawing/tubesheet; calculate free volume = shell minus bundle; include piping to block valves | Shell ID/length within 1 in, thickness within 10 mils UT; volume within 5%; bundle displacement bounded |
| T-07 Historian and meter verification | Quantify heat-input rate over ~3 min heating window | RFI-07 | Quench-water flow through A and total circulation, tube-side pressures, column pressures 24 hr pre-incident through rupture | Export DCS historian for Figures 5-6 window; calibrate/verify orifice/mag meters; interview board operator on 8:33 am actions | Historian CSV with 1-min (or faster) flow/pressure; meter calibration within 5%; timeline to +/-30 s |
| T-08 Procedure and MOC review | Identify procedural/management gap that left B blocked-in and allowed hot-water admission without shell communication | RFI-08 | Feb 2012 cleaning work pack, standby layup instructions, switchover SOP and MOC for 2001 valves | Document review + field operator interviews; compare to PSM/MOC files; note car-seals, blinds, training records | Work order + SOP steps retrieved; layup/drain/vent expectations explicit or confirmed absent |

| RFI-02: Vessel MAWP / hydrotest verification | 300 psig MAWP and 450 psig hydrotest provided only in brief; not quoted in p.6-13 (p.9 indicates withheld detail). Treat as design basis for overpressure margin discussion. | Withheld Section 5.1 and later pages not available; standard vessel documentation not in excerpt. |
| RFI-03: Shell-side isolation status and trapped inventory | p.9 says other reboiler would be offline but ready, isolated by new valves (installed 2001); p.10 confirms gate valves isolating reboilers from fractionator relief valve. Not specified whether Reboiler B shell inlet/outlet block valves were closed trapping liquid propane during 16-month standby, whether drain/vent valves were left open, or whether valves leaked. | ASSUMPTION: Reboiler B shell was blocked-in with liquid propane trapped (both inlet/condensate return and vapor outlet block valves closed, vents/drains closed) for entire 16 months, with no thermal relief. Reason: Described standby "isolated from the process by the new valves" (p.9) and explosion ~3 min after heating implies bulk liquid heating/boiling in sealed volume; leakage would have pressurized to fractionator pressure and not remained liquid-full. | Critical to determine liquid-full hydraulic vs. vapor-space boiling mechanism; no valve position log or P&ID in excerpt. |
| RFI-04: Relief protection elimination | p.10 states isolating valves were not part of original design; Fig.4 schematic (not fully legible) shows isolating reboilers from relief at top of propylene fractionator. Unknown if Reboiler B was connected to any other relief device (e.g., dedicated thermal relief) when isolated, or if operators understood relief was defeated. | ASSUMPTION: When isolated, Reboiler B shell had NO pressure relief path - neither to fractionator overhead PSV nor dedicated thermal PSV. Reason: CSB highlighting of 2001 valves as causal factor (p.10) and fact that vessel ruptured below hydrotest-equivalent pressure indicates absence of adequate relief; original design relied on common column relief. | Relief sizing basis, PSV set pressure, capacity not in p.6-13; need Section 5.1. |
| RFI-05: Fractionator operating pressure and PSV set point | Normal operating pressure of propylene fractionator / reboiler shell not given; PSV set pressure unknown. Affects initial condition before heating (saturated propane at operating T/P vs. atmospheric). | ASSUMPTION: Normal shell-side condition ~130 °F saturated propane (~200 psig vapor pressure at that T). Reason: p.9 shell inlet ~130 °F partially vaporizes propane; typical C3 fractionator reboiler pressure ~150-250 psig; starting point below MAWP but above atmospheric, consistent with 3-min rapid rise to rupture. | No DCS pressure trend in excerpt; need PFD/operating manual. |
| RFI-06: Shell liquid full vs. vapor space condition at heating | Whether Reboiler B shell was 100% liquid-full (hydraulic expansion case) or contained vapor pad (boiling pressure rise limited to vapor pressure at quench temperature) changes predicted pressure by orders of magnitude. | ASSUMPTION: Primary case liquid-full or near-liquid-full with small vapor bubble compressed to nil, leading to hydraulic/near-hydraulic pressure exceeding MAWP within minutes; alternate bounding case saturated propane vapor pressure at quench water temperature (~185-187 F) also exceeds MAWP. Reason: 16-month standstill allows condensation/cooling and accumulation; vessel orientation and trapped condensate scenario typical. | No level, pre-startup vent procedure, or field measurement in excerpt. |
| RFI-07: Propane vapor pressure curve for actual mixture (95% C3 / C4 / C3=) | p.9 mixture ~95% C3; pure propane vapor pressure vs. temperature needed for quantitative pressure estimate; C4s shift bubble point slightly. No tabulated data in excerpt. | ASSUMPTION: Use pure propane saturation properties as proxy; mixture bubble point slightly higher than pure propane but within ~2-5% for 95% purity. Reason: Sufficient for order-of-magnitude demonstration that saturation pressure at 185-187 F exceeds 300 psig MAWP. | Need lab composition or simulator EOS; not in p.6-13. |

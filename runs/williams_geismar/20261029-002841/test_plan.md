# Field test plan (closes RFI items before design)

| test | purpose | closes_rfi | measurements | procedure | acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Verify vessel design pressures and relief design basis | Close RFI-02, RFI-04, RFI-05 | RFI-02, RFI-04, RFI-05 | Photograph nameplates on Reboilers A/B and propylene fractionator; obtain U-1A forms, design datasheets, original vs. 2001 MOC P&IDs (Fig.4 as-built); PSV tags at top of fractionator (set pressure, capacity, sizing calc), any dedicated reboiler thermal relief | Site walk with P&ID mark-up: trace shell inlet (liquid from column bottom), vapor return to column, tube-side quench water in/out block valves, gate valves to relief header. Confirm whether closed isolation defeats relief. Review DCS historian for normal reboiler shell pressure at ~130 F. | MAWP 300 psig / hydro 450 psig confirmed on docs; PSV set ≤ MAWP with capacity ≥ heat-input/thermal expansion case; as-built P&ID matches field locked-open/car-sealed requirements per ASME/API 521 |
| Measure quench water and shell-side operating temperatures and pressures | Close RFI-01, RFI-05, RFI-07 | RFI-01, RFI-05 | Calibrated RTD/thermowell at Reboiler A tube inlet/outlet and Reboiler B tube inlet pipe (quench water), and propylene fractionator bottoms / reboiler shell inlet (130 F zone); pressure transmitters on fractionator overhead and reboiler shells; lab sample of shell fluid for 95% C3 assay | Install temporary gauges or pull DCS PI historian for 24-hr trends before/after incident date; sample bomb from column sump for GC composition; compare saturation pressure at measured T to MAWP using NIST REFPROP / Aspen EOS | Quench water inlet 185-187 F confirmed within ±2 F; shell saturated pressure at 185-187 F >300 psig (demonstrating inherent overpressure); composition 95±2% propane validated |
| Determine trapped-liquid condition and hydraulic overpressure potential | Close RFI-03, RFI-06 | RFI-03, RFI-06 | Valve position survey (open/closed/locked) for Reboiler B shell inlet, vapor return, condensate, vent, drain; borescope for liquid presence; shell volume calc from 18.5 ft x >5 ft dia + tube displacement; liquid thermal expansion coefficient for propane | With unit down and LOTO, verify block valves hold, check if vents/drains were piped to safe location and left open per procedure; witness water-fill test or level gauge reading; calculate pressure rise per deg F for liquid-full blocked-in case (dP/dT ≈ β/κ) and compare to MAWP | Either shell found capable of being blocked liquid-full with no thermal relief path (deficiency confirmed), or documented venting procedure exists that prevents hydraulic lock; dP/dT calc shows exceeding MAWP in <3 min plausible |
| Reconstruct incident timeline and human factors / procedures | Close RFI-03, RFI-08 | RFI-03, RFI-08 | DCS/SIS logs: quench water flow Fig.5/6 trends with time stamps, valve command vs. feedback for Reboiler B quench valves at 8:33 am, alarm log; interviews; SOP for "switching reboilers" and fouling troubleshooting checklist; MOC file for 2001 valve addition with PHA/LOPA | Pull historian at 1-sec resolution around 08:30-08:36 am 6/13/2013; confirm flow increase Fig.6 magnitude; review whether procedure required manager approval, pre-job briefing, gradual warm-up, opening vapor path first; audit training records for supervisor/operators | Timeline 08:33 open to ~08:36 rupture corroborated; absence/inadequacy of formal standby reboiler startup procedure and MOC for relief isolation documented as root cause |
| Non-destructive testing of Reboiler B remnant and Reboiler A analogue | Close RFI-09 and validate failure pressure estimate | RFI-09 | Visual/fractography, UT thickness, hardness, Charpy of shell material; burst pressure calculation per ASME vs. hydrotest 450 psig; comparison to saturation pressure at 187 F (~400-550 psig range for propane) | Lab exam of ruptured sections (Fig.7/8) for ductile overload vs. pre-existing defect; review MT/UT records from Feb 2012 maintenance; calc thin-wall hoop stress at MAWP/hydro/saturation pressure | Fracture consistent with overpressure exceeding MAWP and approaching/exceeding hydrotest, not corrosion/thinning; estimated pressure at rupture bounded by liquid thermal expansion theory and propane vapor pressure at quench temperature |

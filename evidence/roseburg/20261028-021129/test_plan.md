# Field test plan (closes RFI items before design)

| test | purpose | closes_rfi | measurements | procedure | acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raw pump firm retest with cleaned intake | Separate pump degradation (esp Pump 3) from system-head/river effects; prove 14 vs 15 vs 16 mgd firm | RFI-01 | River level, intake differential across trash rack/screens, each raw pump flow (1-4 solo + combos for firm), discharge pressure, amps, TDH | Clean entire intake first (p.109); then run solo + firm combos (largest out) at normal/high river; record mag meter + pressure gauges; compare to 2800/4200 gpm @49 ft | Repeatable within 2% between raw mag meter and calibrated pitot; pump amps within nameplate; river level recorded |
| Raw pipeline headloss survey | Totalize Table 4-1 unit losses to TDH; size higher-head pumps if needed | RFI-02 | Pipe length, diameter, wall, valve/fitting count, upstream/downstream pressures at 3 flows, flow by raw mag meter | Survey 30-in raw alignment vault to basin; log pressures/flows at ~8/12/14 mgd; fit C; check 6 fps threshold near 18 mgd | Length to ±10 ft, Hazen-Williams C within ±5, predicted total loss within 0.2 ft at 12-13 mgd check point |
| Floc/sed water-surface profile | Locate freeboard/weir binding flow; confirm second weir need | RFI-04 | Basin influent channel WS, launder WS/invert, settled channel WS, overflow depth/flow, drain flow at 11-13 mgd | Staff gauges + weir readings during sustained high flow; dye/velocity check split with one vs two basins if possible; measure drain MH weir | Freeboard >=1.5 ft with two basins at 18 mgd; overflow weir flow within 10% of 12 mgd at 9-in; drain 10 mgd confirmed |
| Settled-pipe headloss calibration | Calibrate settled-pipe loss for 18-24 mgd projection and yard-piping shortening design | RFI-05 | Settled channel WS vs filter inlet WS at 2+ flows spanning 10-15 mgd, flow by raw mag meter | Paired WS readings + 42-in length survey and tie-in location; calibrate at one flow, validate at independent flow; report residual ft and % of headloss | Fitted coefficient predicts independent flow within 0.1 ft (≈% of headloss reported); margin to 465.0 overflow confirmed |
| Filter flow split and to-waste capacity | Quantify 7% bias and 8-in ~2 mgd limit; design reconfiguration/meter type change | RFI-07 | Each filter effluent (existing propeller) vs temporary clamp-on/mag reference; to-waste flow by temporary meter; turbidity sample attempts | Run all filters to clearwell then one to waste at 9-12 mgd; record splits; test straight-pipe sensitivity; trial sample-point relocation | Individual filter flows sum within 2% of raw mag meter; to-waste capacity to 3-4 mgd demonstrated with modulation |
| Clearwell volume and backwash drawdown | Separate total 627k/712k from usable; verify 60-min/57-min claims | RFI-08 | Clearwell level vs stored volume (bathymetry), backwash drawdown volume, pump wetwell cutoff, chlorine residual/CT sampling | Level-volume survey of 3 zones + under-filter addition area; log level during backwash; coordinate Sec 3 CT sampling at 446.5/448/449 ft | Usable volume and 1-ft drop confirmed; minimum operating level for CT/wetwell marked; detention calc reconciled |
| High-service firm test | Prove 10 nominal/11.5 true and 18-mgd Table 4-2 addition need | RFI-09 | Each high-service pump flow/head/amps, header pressure, WTP-site main flow (replace propeller check), Reservoir Hill level/receipt | Solo + firm combos per Table 4-2; record system HGL to 710 reservoir; test VFD No.2 control; compare 1400/2800 ratings vs 3300 gpm field point | Firm (largest out) sustains target within 5% at required TDH; VFD holds clearwell ±0.2 ft without throttling |
| Transmission capacity logging | Determine if second main prior/concurrent with 18 mgd | RFI-10 | WTP discharge HGL, intermediate pressures, reservoir inflow/level, parallel main status/lengths | Coordinate with master-plan update; fire-flow/daily-cycle logging at 11-14 mgd; verify 9400-ft Phase 1 and 1600-ft 27-in/30-in status in field | System HGL to Reservoir Hill within model 5%; Phase 1 two-pipe receipt confirmed; Phase 2/second 30-in need decided |
| Surge verification | Size replacement hydropneumatic tank (second impractical) | RFI-11 | Transient pressures at pump station and high points during controlled trip at current max; tank precharge/volume | Surge consultant power-trip test + model to 18 mgd; check space for larger vessel | Upsurge <=150 psi and downsurge >=HGL 630 ft at 18 mgd trip; replacement size recommended |
| Backwash/wastewater/solids mass balance | Prove waste path and 2x300k gal + beds to 18 mgd; trigger 24-to-30-in upsize | RFI-12,RFI-13 | Backwash flow hydrograph (3250-4400 gpm), channel depths, MH weir flow, WWPS run times, basin levels/decant quality, drying bed load | Instrument one backwash cycle + WWPS cycle + basin decant; 24-hr residuals balance at high-flow week; sample settled sludge | Channel/MH/weir pass backwash peak without surcharge; WWPS firm 3000 gpm sustained; basin fill/decant cycle closes |

# Field test plan (closes RFI items before design)

| test | purpose | closes_rfi | measurements | procedure | acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-01 Raw-water pump retest with VFDs commissioned | Close intake firm-capacity conflict (14 vs 15 mgd) and set 18 mgd limit flow | Raw water pump station | River El., forebay El., pump discharge pressure/flow per pump and combinations (N and N-1), motor amps, VFD Hz; river 420-453 ft range | Step-drawdown at low and high river: isolate and run each pump singly, then N-1 combos as Sept 2006 (1,2,4) and 2002; record mag-meter flow vs TDH; inspect Pump 3 impeller/wear ring | Report measured firm Q within 5% of headloss model; report river level, forebay level, discharge pressure; if Pump 3 >10% below curve, flag for rehab |
| T-02 Raw-water pipeline headloss test + survey | Provide pumped-leg TDH for run_hydraulics to 18 mgd and velocity check | 30-in steel raw water pipeline | Upstream/downstream pressures at pump header and basin inlet, mag-meter flow 8-14 mgd, pipe length/inverts from survey, wall thickness verification | Run 3+ flows (e.g., 8, 12, 14 mgd), log headloss vs flow, survey profile, include meter loss; fit C-factor | Hazen-Williams C calibrated within +/-10; modeled vs measured headloss residual <0.5 ft at 12 mgd |
| T-03 Pretreatment geometry + performance test | Separate hydraulic limit (weir/max WS) from treatment limit (overflow rate) for 18 mgd | Single floc/sed basin rated 12 mgd | Basin LxWxD, floc stages, settled-water launder lengths/weir settings, sludge blanket, turbidity in/out at warm and cold events, water El. vs flow | Dimension survey + 24-hr flow-paced sampling at current peak (~11-12 mgd) and if possible controlled 14+ mgd; record weir submergence and freeboard | Report detention, overflow rate, weir loading at 12 and 18 mgd; freeboard to 465.00/465.98 within 0.1 ft; chain-flight solids flux noted |
| T-04 Synoptic hydraulic grade survey (gravity train) | Build run_profile treatment train and find gravity-limit flows to 18 mgd | Settled water conveyance; Profile calibration | Water Els. at sed launders, settled channel, filter influent channel, filter inlet (463-464 ft band), filter effluent, clearwell 438.5-449.5 ft; flows per filter | Synoptic level survey at two independent flows (e.g., ~9 mgd calibration, ~11-12 mgd validation); do not reuse calibration point for validation; survey inverts | Calibrated gravity model residual <0.2 ft at validation flow; % of headloss reported; limiting flow where freeboard =0 identified |
| T-05 Filter loading and N-1 test | Set filter treatment limit flow and need for Filters 5-6 for 18 mgd | Four filters + space for Filters 5-6 | Filter areas, media depth/size, run time, headloss, effluent turbidity, flow per filter with one filter in backwash/waste | Filter loading test: run N and N-1 configurations to max available flow; log per-filter flows and effluent quality; confirm 20-in inlet valve losses | Report gpm/sf at 12 and 18 mgd for N and N-1; note breakthrough/shear events; if N-1 loading exceeds state limit, filter addition required |
| T-06 Filter-to-waste capacity test | Prove least-cost fix (meter/sample vs full reconfig) for 18 mgd | Filter effluent and filter-to-waste piping | Waste flow vs valve position, filter level 463-464 ft excursion, effluent meter accuracy, sample-point turbidity during waste | Route each filter to waste at design rate; measure waste Q by temporary meter/drawdown; test existing 2-mgd ceiling and throttled vs unthrottled response | Demonstrate waste >= filter design flow (e.g., 3-4.5 mgd) with level control +/-0.2 ft; turbidity sample valid during waste |
| T-07 Clearwell usable-volume + CT verification (with Section 3 data) | Resolve storage vs CT conflict for 18 mgd | Clearwell disinfection contact time | Clearwell bathymetry, operating Els. 8-10 ft depth, backwash drawdown volume 65-75k gal, chlorine residual, tracer or Section 3 CT calcs | Level logging through backwash cycle + CT calculation per Section 3; dye/tracer if required to prove baffling factor for baffled 324k-gal zone | Usable volume and CT ratio >=1.0 at 18 mgd band 8-10 ft with 1-ft backwash drawdown; if not, baffling/level fix scoped |
| T-08 HSPS + transmission pump/system test | Set HSPS limit flow and size spare pump(s)/higher-head option for 18 mgd | High service pumps + WTP-to-Hill transmission | HS pump pressures/flows singly and combined, header loss, on-site 30-in (29.25-in ID) loss, off-site 20-in +30-in pressures to Hill, reservoir Els. | Pump/system test at 3+ total flows to current max; record VFD Hz vs Q-H; coordinate with City for Hill reservoir levels and parallel-main status | System curve to Reservoir El.710 ft within 5 ft TDH; firm Q with largest HS pump out reported; VFD turndown noted |
| T-09 Surge trip test + model to 18 mgd | Lift 14-mgd surge limit for 18 mgd pumping | 1900 cf surge tank rated to 14.0 mgd | Pressures at HSPS and high points during pump trip at max testable flow; tank precharge/volume, air inventory | Controlled trip test + calibrated surge model extended to 18 mgd (12500 gpm); evaluate tank upsizing or additional vessel | Transient model shows upsurge <=150 psi and downsurge >=HGL 630 ft at 18 mgd; if exceeded, tank upgrade sized |
| T-10 Backwash-to-basin mass/hydraulic test | Prove waste-handling train to 18 mgd wash frequency | Backwash pumps/piping and wastewater return | Backwash Q/duration per filter, 16-in header loss, 16/24/30-in drain hydraulics, WWPS inflow/levels, basin fill/decant rates | Time full backwash cycle per filter; log WWPS wetwell and basin Els.; test deep-bed rate if media change planned (4400 gpm target vs 4060 demonstrated) | Report equalization adequacy: WWPS firm 3000 gpm vs backwash 3250-4400 gpm with basin levels; no sanitary overflow to river at 18-mgd wash frequency |

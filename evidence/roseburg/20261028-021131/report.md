# Technical Memorandum – Winchester Water Treatment Plant 12 mgd to 18 mgd Expansion Screening

## Purpose
Determine whether the Winchester Water Treatment Plant, Roseburg, Oregon can be expanded from its current 12 mgd to 18 mgd. For each unit along the flow path, determine whether it limits 18 mgd, the flow at which it becomes limiting, and the least-cost modification that makes 18 mgd work.

Source: City of Roseburg, Water Treatment Facilities Preliminary Design Report, July 2009 (public record) – https://roseburgor.gov/storage/app/media/PWD/Public%20Works%20Images%20and%20Docs/Water%20Treatment%20Facilities%20Preliminary%20Design%20Report.pdf

## Background
- Current plant nominal treatment rating: 12 mgd single flocculation/sedimentation basin rated at 12 mgd (p.111).
- Expansion target flow for this study: 18 mgd expanded plant capacity with two additional filters (Filters 5 and 6) (p.115).
- Drawing ultimate design flow: 22 MGD ultimate plant flow note on WINCHESTER WTP HYDRAULIC PROFILE (dwg p.136).
- Ultimate overflow / planning flow: 24 mgd combined clearwell overflows can accommodate ultimate plant flow (p.114).
- Plant operating range observed: 3.5 mgd low demand to 11.6 mgd recent peak summer day, operated 24/7 to keep Reservoir Hill terminal reservoirs full (p.108).
- Demonstrated pretreatment performance: Excellent pretreatment at sustained flows up to 11.5 mgd warm low-turbidity; normal cold high-turbidity 3.5-5.0 mgd (p.111).
- Plant operating range: 3.5 to 5.0 mgd normal during high turbidity, colder water conditions (p.111).

Flow path units evaluated (`units_on_path`):
River intake and raw water pump station; 30-in steel raw water pipeline + 30-in mag meter vault to floc/sed basin NE corner; Rapid mix (dwg 466.0 WS / 467.5 wall); Flocculation basin (invert 454.0); Sedimentation basin (WS 464.83 / OF WEIR 465.00 / MAX WS 465.98 / invert 454.0) + chain-and-flight + settled-water launders; Settled-water collection channel and pipe to filters; Filter influent channel + per-filter 20-in inlet + isolation valve; Filters Nos.1-4 (operating 463-464 ft; 5-6 ft submergence) + space for Filters 5-6 (+85k gal clearwell under); Filter effluent piping + flow control system + meters; Filter-to-waste piping + sample point (existing ~2 mgd ceiling); Clearwell Zones 1-3 (170k + 324k baffled +133k =627k gal at 10.5 ft; invert 438.5); Clearwell overflows: HSPS weir 449.5x9 ft (~19 mgd at 1 ft) + 4.5x4.5 chamber +18-in river outfall; Filter No.3 weir 450.00x15 ft; combined 24 mgd; High service pump station (1x1400 gpm +3x2800 gpm at 305 ft TDH; VFD on No.2; +2 spaces) + 18-to-30-in header; On-site 30-in finished water main (29.25-in ID, 3/8-in wall) +30-in propeller meter +1900 cf surge tank; Off-site transmission to Reservoir Hill (20-in steel +30-in DI; Phase 9,400 ft parallel; 27-in/24-in reach; future 30-in + second 30-in) to El.710 ft reservoirs; Air scour (1050 scfm at 6.4 psi, 60 hp, 8-in supply); Backwash pumps (2x3250 gpm at 41 ft TDH, 50 hp, 16-in header) + per-filter 16-in drains + channel +24-in to MH +30-in to WWPS; Wastewater pump station (3x1500 gpm at 40 ft, 35 hp, 10-in discharges, 16-in transfer, 3000 gpm firm) + backwash waste channel/diversion MH; Washwater/solids equalization basins 1-2 (2x300k gal with decants) + 2 drying beds; overflow to river 451.0.

Disagreement with analysis text stated explicitly:
- W-PS-02 states “Raw water pumping station” with basis HSPS-6PUMP, but analysis runs HSPS-EXIST / HSPS-6PUMP are High service pump station on-site screening only via 29.25-in ID (p.116), omitting off-site 20-in +30-in, 9400 ft parallel, 27/24-in reach (p.118), per review. The rule label and basis therefore do not match the modeled unit.
- W-PS-03 reports pass 1.19 for HSPS-6PUMP firm 21.43 mgd vs 18 mgd, but analysis review finds HSPS firm capacities are on-site screening only with single-point EPANET curves (screening only) and do not prove deliverable flow to El.710 ft reservoirs (dwg p.136) through total system headloss p.117-p.118. The pass is therefore CONDITIONAL on Test T-08 total-system verification, not a final compliance finding.
- W-SED-01 FAIL is consistent with analysis finding that single basin rated 12 mgd (p.111) binds at 12.0 mgd treatment rating.

# Field test plan (closes RFI items before design)

| test | purpose | closes_rfi | measurements | procedure | acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-RWPS: raw-water pump station TDH-Q test | Establish rated vs field firm capacity and TDH at 18 mgd with Table 4-1 HL | Raw water pumps | River level, pump suction/discharge pressure, flow by 30-in mag meter + clamp-on check, amps/volts, VFD Hz, valve positions, pump combo (including N-1 combos and Pump 3 alone) | Step-test each pump alone then combos 1-2-4, 1-2-3, etc. at normal and low river stage; record TDH vs Q; compare 2002 15.0 mgd and 2006 14 mgd baselines; inspect Pump 3 wear/clearance | Pump Q within 5% of curve at measured TDH; firm (N-1) >=18 mgd required to clear RFI; if <18 mgd intake/pumps limit confirmed |
| T-INT: intake approach-velocity survey | Find Q at 0.4 fps binding limit | Intake fish-velocity capacity | River stage, gate openings/submergence, velocity traverse at each 4x4-ft opening and at screens, trash-rack differential, flow (plant mag meter) | ADCP/velocity meter traverse across 4 openings at 3 plant flows up to max available; compute Q/area vs 0.4 fps; survey opening inverts and low-water submergence | Approach velocity <=0.4 fps at 18 mgd demand with gates fully submerged; else intake limits and Sec 6 expansion required |
| T-PIPE: raw + finished site-main HL test | Calibrate run_hydraulics pumped legs and verify 5.9/5.97 fps at 18 mgd | Pipeline HL calibration | Differential pressure/flow over known length for 30-in raw and 30-in finished site main at 3 flows; record temperature, valve/meter losses | Pressure gauges upstream/downstream of test reach + mag meter flow; 30-min steady at each flow; compute ft/100ft vs Table values | HL per 100 ft within 15% of Table 4-1/4-3 at test flow; calibrated C/length then used for 18-mgd TDH; velocity >6.0 fps flags modification |
| T-SED: sed-basin levels + 42-in HL test | Calibrate run_profile gravity leg and find Q where OF/freeboard binds; confirm 22-24 mgd pipe limit | Flocculation/sedimentation performance and levels; Settled water pipeline calibration | Influent-channel WS, settled-channel WS, launder/trough WS, filter level, settled 42-in flow and differential HL, overflow weir/drain flow if any | Staged flow test (if available to ~13-14 mgd single basin + model to 9 mgd/basin two-basin); survey weir/trough inverts (resolve 465.83 vs 464.63 conflict) and wall tops; dye/tracer for short-circuiting | Levels stable below OF 465.0 with >=1.0 ft margin to filter level and >=1.5 ft freeboard at 18 mgd (2 basins); HL vs 2.8-in at 13.1 mgd baseline consistent |
| T-FLT: filter loading + FTW modulation test | Determine firm filtration capacity and Q-limit to 18 mgd; size added area or high-rate media need | Filters, filter effluent and filter-to-waste piping | Per-filter flow, influent channel level (463-464 ft datum), effluent turbidity/particle counts, headloss, backwash rate/duration, FTW flow/modulation, valve timing | Load test at max available flow with one filter in FTW; step flows; record >3.4 mgd/filter behavior noted p.113; survey media depth/type and measure area | Loading <= approved gpm/sf with one filter out and one in FTW (~2 mgd waste per p.113); turbidity/breakthrough criteria met; else filters limit 18 mgd |
| T-CT: clearwell detention/CT test | Verify disinfection at 18 mgd, not just volume | Clearwell contact time | Clearwell levels, in/out flows, chlorine residual in/out, temperature/pH, tracer or level-volume curve to verify 170k/324k/133k gal at 10.5 ft | Volume survey + flow-through test; compute detention at 12 vs 18 mgd (75 min baseline p.114); coordinate with State for baffling factor and required CT | CT ratio >=1.0 at 18 mgd through baffled volume at operating depth 446.5-448.0 ft with maintained chlorine residual; else clearwell/CT limits |
| T-HS: high-service + transmission + surge test | Find HSPS/transmission limiting Q and define least-cost pump/pipe/controls fix for 18 mgd | High service pumps + WTP-to-Hill transmission; Surge control to 18 mgd | HS pump suction/discharge pressure, flow, power/VFD Hz per combo (N-1), transmission pressures along route, reservoir levels, surge-tank level/pressure on pump trip/startup | HS pump TDH-Q step test + controlled trip to record upsurge/downsurge; compare to 9,700 gpm/14.0 mgd rating; log VFD ramp capability | Discharge pressure/HGL to Reservoir Hill (710 ft) meets system curve to 18 mgd with N-1 firm; surge within 150 psi / HGL 630 ft to 18 mgd or mitigation defined |
| T-BW: backwash + wastewater + basins cycle test | Prove backwash/wastewater/solids chain to 18 mgd and size any WWPS/basin increment | Wastewater/solids handling to 18 mgd | Backwash flow (4,060 gpm baseline at 45-deg), header/channel levels, drain MH levels, WWPS wetwell in/out, pump run times, basin fill/decant volumes (300k gal each) | Witness full backwash cycle per filter; throttle test toward 4,400 gpm; monitor WWPS + basins through 2 cycles; time basin turnaround | Backwash rate >= design for existing media and >=4,400 gpm if deep-bed (1.1 mm) pursued; drain channel/24-in/30-in convey without flooding; WWPS firm 3,000 gpm keeps up with sequence at 18-mgd production |
| T-SURV: elevation confirmation survey | Provide confirmed datums to calibrate run_profile at drawing design flow 22 mgd | Hydraulic profile drawing elevations | Level-loop survey of all DR-DWG-02 to 05 points: intake inverts/staff, rapid-mix/sed weirs/troughs/walls, filter weirs/channels, clearwell floor/weirs, WWPS/diversion MH/basins, reservoir overflow | Closed-loop levels from plant benchmark; read twice independently per drawing rules; photograph nameplates/weir plates | All single-read dwg elevations confirmed within 0.1 ft by survey/level loop; drawing_ws_ft at 22-mgd basis reconciled to text (OF 465.0, filter 463-464, clearwell 449.0) with residuals reported |
| T-CHEM: chemical feed capacity check | Close chemical unknown for 18-mgd feasibility | Chemical systems | Feeder max/min, tankage, dosing vs flow, SCADA limits for ACH/NaOCl/pH/carbon/filter-aid polymer | Nameplate + drawdown test at current max; extrapolate to 18 mgd; check injection/mixing points on dwg | Inventory of feed-pump max gpd, storage, and control range >=18-mgd dose at low/high turbidity; else chemical limit listed with upgrade cost |

# Field test plan (closes RFI items before design)

| test | purpose | closes_rfi | measurements | procedure | acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-01 DCS and video time-alignment audit | Verify curtailment transient ROC and failure sequence | Absolute vs relative timing; DCS values night of incident | Export raw DCS historian (1-s), event log, PLC clocks, surveillance DVR timestamps; record sensor tag list for Fig.42 metal-T and A/B dP | At control room/historian: pull 10:30 p.m.-11:30 p.m. June 27 trends for ACSR/AWSR; photograph clock offsets; cross-correlate video flash frame to dP drop; re-plot Fig.42 with raw values | Match CSB-corrected timeline within +/-60 s and confirm ACSR precedes AWSR; if AWSR precedes, reopen initial-point hypothesis |
| T-02 ROC re-analysis from historian | Quantify thermal-fatigue loading vs ALPEMA/Chart/GPA/API 662 limits | Metal vs fluid ROC ratio; night-of-incident ROC | Inlet, outlet, and external metal temperatures on ACSR/AWSR at >=1/min resolution, Jan 2011-June 2016 plus night of incident | Compute ROC histograms per Fig.28/29 bins (1.8-3.6, 3.6-12.6, 12.6-21.6, 21.6-30.6, >30.6 degF/min); normalize to A-Train run time; compare metal vs fluid attenuation | Calculate per-minute ROC for each stream and metal tag; exceedance of 3.6 degF/min (transient) or 1.8 degF/min (steady) flags integrity-window violation |
| T-03 Field breach inventory and fractography triage | Confirm ACSR as initial point and progression | Breach count conflict; ACSR origin layer/pass | Visual + dye-penetrant of ACSR core exterior, headers, nozzles, side/cap sheets; map each breach location and fracture surface | Walk A-Train cryogenic area with P&IDs: tag each breach 1-13, photograph fracture faces before cleaning, retain coupons for SEM; distinguish primary fatigue rupture from secondary fire/overpressure tears | Document all 12 (or 13) breaches with photos, P&ID mark-up, and failure-mode label (ductile rupture vs fire damage vs fatigue origin); reconcile count |
| T-04 NDE of repairs and blocked-pass vents | Assess repair quality and trapped-fluid risk | Vent-hole status; internal design details | NDE of ACSR remnant and sister exchangers (B-cold, A/B-warm): external PT, UT shear-wave of side/cap sheets, borescope of blocked passes 97-99 type and ACSR pass 10 sealing | At laydown/cold box: PT all prior weld repairs (Fig.49-52 locations), UT around attachment plates, verify vent holes sealed vs silicone-vented by pick test and pressure vent check; record nameplate, U-stamp, dummy-layer drawings | UT thickness / phased-array indication of disbonds or cracks > acceptance per owner spec; any internal crack in blocked-pass wall fails |
| T-05 Interpass leak (pressure-communication) test | Test LBB assumption that leaks are detectable/benign | Which cracks were LOC; LBB validity | Helium/N2 pressure-hold and leak rate of each pass on spare/sister BAHX; sniff for interpass leakage | Isolate passes, pressurize one pass to operating dP, monitor adjacent/blocked pass pressure for 4 h; use mass-spec leak detection at cap/side-bar welds | If internal leak rate >0 or blocked-layer pressure rises on N2 test, blocked-pass containment is compromised - requires replacement not re-weld |
| T-06 Flow-path and procedure walkdown | Define system boundary for PHA/MOC review | Flow-path completeness | P&ID walkdown, valve lineup for A-Train curtailment/shut-in procedure actually used at 10:50-11:22 p.m., storage-bullet levels | Field verify each unit on path, photograph nameplates, interview board/outside operators on curtailment steps; copy operating procedure and DCS set-point changes | Obtain P&ID-verified lineup: slug catcher -> dehydrator/dust collector -> warm separator -> turboexpander (-90F) / JT -> cold separator -> demethanizer with ACSR/AWSR reboiling -> NGL bullets -> Tri-States; residue -> Destin; condensate -> truck |

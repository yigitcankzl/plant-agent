# Field test plan (closes RFI items before design)

| test | purpose | closes_rfi | measurements | procedure | acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TP-03 Mechanical verification - nameplate, dossier and PRV bench review | Verify provisional 300/450 psig limits and whether isolated shell had any relief before any pressure comparison (RB-PRESS-01/02). | RFI-03, RFI-07 | Shell MAWP, design temp, hydrotest, serial; PRV setpoint, orifice, capacity, inlet/outlet sizes | At Reboiler A (survivor) and B remnant laydown + fractionator top: photograph nameplates/U-stamps, copy U-1/MDR and PRV datasheets + bench-test records; trace 2001 MOC for gate/ball valves; check for thermal reliefs or car-seals. | Nameplate photo matches U-1: MAWP within +/-5 psi of 300 psig, hydrotest 450 psig; PRV tag matches datasheet setpoint; FAIL = mismatch triggers re-rating |
| TP-06 Valve lineup as-found survey | Prove blocked-in volume with no PRV path at rupture. | RFI-06 | Open/closed position of B shell inlet gate, B vapor-return gate, B tube in/out balls, A equivalents, any PRV block valves; stem turns, chain/lock status | In field at fractionator reboiler deck: record stem position, photograph, tag; download DCS/SIS valve feedback and operator logs Feb 2012-June 2013; interview board/field operators on switching procedure used. | Valve stems/handwheels as-found photographed + DCS historian shows B shell gates closed and tube balls opened at 08:33 +/-2 min; any open relief path invalidates blocked-in model |
| TP-05 Inventory and geometry - drain, sample and dimensional | Close inventory + volume for trapped-liquid heating / vapor-pressure runs RB-ISO-01, RB-PRESS-01/02, RB-VOL-01. | RFI-04, RFI-05, RFI-10 | Shell residual composition (GC for propane/propylene/C4s/water), liquid level/drain volume, shell ID/length, tube OD/count for displacement | At Reboiler A (proxy) and B remnant: strap/UT shell ID, tape length, count/measure tubes; attempt controlled drain with level; sample to lab bomb for GC; review Feb 2012 nitrogen-charging and leak-through records. | Drain sample lab shows >80% C3s confirming propane charge; measured liquid level + shell ID allow free-volume calc within 10%; if nitrogen only, hydraulic-burst model rejected |
| TP-09 Thermal-hydraulic historian retrieval + instrument check | Provide initial/boundary conditions for heat-input transient without computing energy here. | RFI-02, RFI-08, RFI-09 | Quench-water inlet/outlet T to A/B, shell-side T/P in B (if transmitter existed), FT/FQ quench flows (Fig.5/6 numeric), ambient T | At DCS historian + field FT (Fig.4 FT/FO), quench pumps: export 1-min (or faster) trends June 12-13, 2013; calibrate check RTDs/FTs in lab; note fouling-reduced flow through A vs jump on B opening. | Calibrated historian export: quench inlet 185-187 F confirmed +/-2 F, shell inlet ~130 F, flows timestamped to 08:33 opening and 08:36 rupture; gaps flagged |

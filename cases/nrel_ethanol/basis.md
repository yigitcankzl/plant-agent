# NREL 2011 cellulosic ethanol plant (corn stover, 2,000 dry tonnes/day)

Source: Humbird et al., NREL/TP-5100-47764 (May 2011).

The plant model is the join of its area models, each rebuilt separately from that area's design basis (A300, A500). Streams carry the report's numbers; a stream leaving one area and entering a later one is connected, and a recycle back to an earlier area is held at its documented value.

# Michigan NGL depropanizer: blind operating basis

Source: Golden and Martin, *Oil & Gas Journal*, August 2003, tower history, original packing performance and project guarantee. This sheet stops before the installed tray arrangement and later troubleshooting changes.

## Existing tower and operation

- The NGL depropanizer operates near **195 psig** and separates propane overhead from butane-and-heavier bottoms.
- Since 1986, the stripping section has contained a **21 ft 9 in.** bed of **1 in. random packing** in place of the original trays. Plant tests indicate about **8-9 theoretical stages**, or **28-32 in. HETP**, in that bed.
- The upper feed location saves roughly **5% steam** relative to the lower feed location.
- Before the later project, feed rate was around **25,000 b/d**. The project guarantee was **30,500 b/d**.
- Design feed contained about **58% propane**, but the plant experiences meaningful composition changes.

## Modelling boundary

- The article does not publish a complete feed assay. Use a C2/C3/C4+ surrogate and label the composition and b/d-to-mass conversion as assumptions.
- Preserve the shell and **195 psig** pressure. Represent the existing packed stripping section and both feed locations.
- Check section flooding separately; an overall column flood number can hide feed-zone liquid backup.

## Project premise

Reach **30,500 b/d** while preserving propane recovery and product quality across feed-composition changes. Determine whether packing, trays or the feed transition controls, and define internals that fit the existing shell.

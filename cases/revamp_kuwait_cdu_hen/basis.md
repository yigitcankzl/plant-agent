# Kuwait atmospheric CDU heat-exchanger network: blind operating basis

Source: Al-Riyami et al., *Case Studies in Thermal Engineering* 28 (2021) 101030, existing-network and pinch-analysis sections. This sheet excludes the candidate retrofit layouts.

## Existing crude preheat system

- The atmospheric unit belongs to a Kuwait refinery processing more than **400,000 b/d** across the refinery complex. The paper does not disclose the exact unit feed rate or crude assay.
- The CDU preheat system contains **19 heat exchangers** arranged in three networks around a preflash vessel.
- Crude leaves the first network at about **128 C**, the second at about **226 C**, passes through the preflash vessel, and leaves the third network at about **265 C** before entering the furnace.
- The furnace raises crude to about **365 C**. Reported existing hot-utility duty is **93 MW** and cold-utility duty is **75.5 MW**.
- The pinch study uses a minimum approach temperature of **12.5 C**.

## Published existing duties used as anchors

- Selected existing exchanger duties are E1 **5.45 MW**, E2 **6.34 MW**, E3 **1.81 MW**, C1 **3.24 MW**, C2 **21.27 MW** and E0 **19.27 MW**.
- Complete stream heat-capacity-flow data are in the source tables, but the crude composition needed for a rigorous petroleum-property model is not disclosed.
- A DWSIM representation may use heat-capacity surrogate streams. Keep the distinction between a heat-balance match and a calibrated crude model.

## Project premise

Reduce furnace fuel and cooling-water duty with the fewest practical changes to the existing network. Respect the **12.5 C** minimum approach and report every relocated exchanger, new exchanger area and changed train temperature.

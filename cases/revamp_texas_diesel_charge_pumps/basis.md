# Texas diesel-hydrotreater charge pumps: blind operating basis

Source: a vendor field report. The citation is withheld from the agent because it identifies the outcome; it is recorded with the withheld outcome in `outcome.json` and in the case's `source` field.

## Existing pumps

- Two multistage BB5 barrel pumps provide diesel-hydrotreater charge service at a Texas refinery.
- Each original pump has **10 stages** and is rated for **1,110 US gpm** (**252 m3/h**) at **4,014 ft** (**1,223 m**) differential head.
- The installed barrel, suction and discharge nozzles, foundation and piping are to remain in place.
- The publication does not provide a complete diesel assay, NPSH curve, driver curve or original efficiency map.

## Required duty and modelling boundary

- The required new operating point is **1,628 US gpm** (**370 m3/h**) at **3,450 ft** (**1,052 m**) head: approximately **47% more flow** but less head than the original rating.
- Build a diesel-surrogate pump duty and check hydraulic power, assumed efficiency, line velocity and available differential pressure.
- Treat impeller diameter, number of stages, internal clearances and casing passage area as physical ratings. A vendor must still verify rotor dynamics, NPSH, pressure containment and the final curve.

## Project premise

Reach the new flow and head while retaining the installed barrel, footprint and plant piping. Find the smallest pump-internal scope; do not solve the problem by editing the existing rating without a physical modification.
